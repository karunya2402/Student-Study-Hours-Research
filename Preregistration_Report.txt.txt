PREREGISTRATION REPORT

Project Title:
Student Study Hours vs Exam Performance

1. Research Question:

Is there a relationship between the number of hours students study and their exam scores?

2. Population:

Students.

3. Exposure:

Number of hours spent studying.

4. Comparator:

Students who study fewer hours compared with students who study more hours.

5. Outcome:

Students' exam scores (0–100).

6. Observation Window:

One examination period.

7. Research Hypotheses:

Null Hypothesis (H0):
There is no significant relationship between study hours and exam scores.

Alternative Hypothesis (H1):
There is a significant relationship between study hours and exam scores.


8. Data Exclusion Criteria:

The following data will be excluded:

1. Missing values.
2. Negative study hours.
3. Exam scores below 0.
4. Exam scores above 100.

Only valid observations will be included in the analysis.


9. Statistical Analysis Plan:

The dataset will be analyzed using Python.

Libraries:
1. Pandas – Data handling.
2. Matplotlib – Data visualization.
3. SciPy – Statistical analysis.

Analysis Methods:
1. Display the dataset.
2. Check missing values.
3. Calculate descriptive statistics.
4. Create a scatter plot.
5. Calculate the Pearson correlation coefficient.
6. Perform the Pearson correlation test.

Significance Level:
The significance level will be set at 0.05.


10. Expected Results and Limitations:

Expected Results:
We expect a positive relationship between study hours and exam scores.

Students who study more hours may achieve higher exam scores.

Limitations:
1. The dataset contains only 10 observations.
2. The dataset is synthetic, not real student data.
3. The results cannot be generalized to all students.
4. Correlation does not prove causation.
